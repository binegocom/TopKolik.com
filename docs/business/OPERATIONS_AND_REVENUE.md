# Advertising, accounting, newsroom and support workflows

## Advertising inventory
Define web placements (hero, in-feed, article sidebar/inline, footer), app placements (in-feed native placements, interstitial only if platform-compatible), sponsor labels, frequency caps and user experience constraints. Separate brand-safe sponsored content from editorial news. Ban undisclosed paid editorial claims and gambling promos until legal/store approval.

## Campaign life cycle
Advertiser -> business verification -> contract -> media/creative upload -> malware and policy checks -> editorial/ad-ops approval -> audience/sport placement -> schedule/budget pacing -> serving -> event dedupe -> billing/report -> close/refund. Scope every ad impression, click and report by advertiser_id. Conversions only where consent and privacy allow.

## Finance
Tax jurisdiction and entity approvals required before issuing real invoices. Approved chart of accounts, double-entry ledger, invoices, credit notes, payments, adjustments, refunds, revenue recognition, reconciliation, invoice numbering, VAT treatment and export; restrict journal edits with dual approval and immutable audit. Use a payment service only after commercial and compliance selection.

## Newsroom
Publisher, managing editor, editor, AI ops, sports data ops; each has own dashboard, queue, limits and audit. No uncontrolled AI auto-publish on untrusted data. Editors may quarantine, revise, retract and pause publication policies.

## Support
Tickets by authenticated reader or guest contact method, categories, SLA/priority, assignment, internal notes, user messages, spam safeguards, PII access logging, archival/retention, blocked content escalation, and incident linking.

## Analytics
Privacy-respecting event schema for visit, article read, search, favorites, push subscription, match detail view, ad viewability/valid impression, advertiser report and article provenance quality. Segment by consent and retention policy; no silent cross-domain identity stitching.

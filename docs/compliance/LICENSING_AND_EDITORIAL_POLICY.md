# Rights and editorial publication policy

## Legal prerequisites
Before connecting a sports provider in production, obtain contract rights to display, cache, derive, distribute and publish the specific fixture/statistics/standings dataset on web and mobile. Verify geographic limits, retention, attribution and sublicensing. General public visibility on Maçkolik/Sofascore does not grant a scraping license.

## Image and branding rights
Do not copy licensed photographs, club crests, league marks or player portraits without appropriate rights. An AI-generated image must be labelled if realistic but synthetic and must not falsely portray an actual event. Keep original sources, rights and takedown channels.

## Editorial trust
Attribution, publication timestamp, correction history, human publisher identification, accessible contact and complaints route. Clear automated generation disclosure. Automatically quarantine contested factual claims, unconfirmed transfers, injuries and allegations. Maintain a kill switch for sources/classes.

## Platform regulations
Review Google Play News & Magazines policies, Apple App Review (including rights and minimum functionality), Google Search scaled-content spam and structured-data rules, Turkish publication/advertising/privacy rules, KVKK, consumer protections, advertising and tax requirements. Obtain specialist legal advice before launch.
